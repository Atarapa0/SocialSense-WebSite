Data Deletion Instructions

Effective date: October 3, 2026

SocialSense analyzes supported Instagram data export archives on your device. Your Instagram ZIP archive is not uploaded to SocialSense-controlled servers for analysis. Local analysis records, original ZIP files, temporary copies, backups, and support communications are handled separately, as explained below.

1. Local Analysis Data

SocialSense may store parsed analysis data locally on your device so that the archive does not have to be analyzed again each time the Application is opened.

If a data-clearing function is available in your version of SocialSense, it is intended to clear the Application’s primary local analysis record and associated last-update information. Use the data-management options available in your version; the exact labels may vary.

Clearing the analysis record may not remove all Application preferences, such as theme or language, every temporary ZIP copy, the original Instagram ZIP file, or backups.

2. Original Instagram ZIP File

SocialSense does not automatically delete your original Instagram ZIP file stored in Files, Downloads, iCloud Drive, or another location outside the Application’s storage.

If you want to delete that original file, delete it separately from the location where you saved it, using your device’s file manager or the relevant storage service.

3. Temporary ZIP Files

Your operating system or system file picker may create a temporary local copy of the ZIP file you select. The creation, retention, and deletion of these temporary files may be managed in part by the operating system and file-picker components.

Do not assume that clearing the local analysis record also deletes every temporary copy.

4. Uninstalling the Application and Backups

Uninstalling SocialSense may cause data in the Application’s storage to be removed as managed by your operating system. It does not guarantee that all associated data is immediately deleted.

Your original Instagram ZIP file may remain in your separate file storage. Backups may also remain subject to the rules and settings of your operating system or the relevant external service. Manage those files and backups separately if you want to remove them.

5. Account Deletion

The current version of SocialSense does not have a SocialSense registration or user-account system. There is therefore no remote SocialSense user account to delete.

Managing SocialSense’s local data is separate from managing or deleting your Instagram account.

6. Email and Support Information

If you have contacted us by email, we may process your email address, name or display name, message content, attachments you voluntarily provide, and standard email metadata to handle your request.

To request deletion of support information retained by us, email furkaan.er.34@gmail.com. Please do not send your password or sensitive Instagram archives with your deletion request.

We will process applicable deletion requests within the period required by applicable law.

Records that must be retained by law may be excluded from deletion. A support request does not remotely clear analysis records or files stored only on your device.

7. More Information

For details about local processing, website infrastructure, external services, retention, and your rights, please read the Privacy Policy.
