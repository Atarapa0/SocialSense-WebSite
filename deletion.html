Data Deletion

Last updated: November 8, 2026

In case of any conflict, the Turkish version prevails.

In short

SocialSense has no accounts, and the developer’s servers hold no data about you. There is therefore no account or record to ask the developer to delete. All App data is only on your device, and you can delete it yourself.

Deleting App data

Uninstalling the App removes all of its data from your device.

  • iPhone: Touch and hold the SocialSense icon on the Home Screen, tap “Remove App”, then “Delete App”.
  • Android: Touch and hold the SocialSense icon and choose “Uninstall”, or go to Settings > Apps > SocialSense > Uninstall.

Uploading a new file replaces the data from the previous one.

Backups

If iCloud or Google backup is on, App data may remain in those backups. They are in your own account, and you can delete them from your device’s backup settings. On Android, data may be restored from a backup if you reinstall the App; to prevent this, go to Settings > Apps > SocialSense > Storage > Clear data before uninstalling.

The ZIP file you downloaded

The file you downloaded from Instagram stays in its download folder (for example, Downloads) after you upload it. It may contain private information, so we recommend deleting it when you no longer need it.

If you emailed us

Email correspondence is deleted within 15 days after your request is resolved. To have it deleted sooner, write to info@furkanerdogan.com.

You can also send your request via the contact form.

Your Instagram account

SocialSense does not access your Instagram account or your data on Instagram. To delete your data on Instagram, use Instagram’s own settings.
