SocialSense Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 8, 2026

In case of any conflict, the Turkish version prevails.

1. Summary

The Developer does not collect any personal data through SocialSense. The file you upload is processed only on your device and is never sent to any server. The developer has no access to this data. This policy covers the SocialSense mobile app (“App”), published by Furkan Erdoğan, an individual developer (“Developer”). Contact: info@furkanerdogan.com

2. How the App works

You do not need an account or a login. You download the data export file (ZIP, in JSON format) for your own account from Instagram, upload it to the App with your device’s file picker, and the App reads it on your device to calculate statistics. The file may contain information about you and other people, such as follower and following lists, likes, comments and messages. All of it is processed only on your device. The App does not send the file or its contents to the Developer or to any server.

3. Network and third parties

The App contains no analytics, advertising, crash reporting or similar third-party services, and it does not share or sell any data about you. The App Store and Google Play may collect some information under their own policies when you download and use the App (such as download counts, device model, or crash reports if you allow them); this information is governed by Apple’s and Google’s privacy policies, and through them the Developer can only see aggregated statistics and, if you allow it, crash reports that do not identify you (such as device model, OS version and error log). Some links in the App (for example, the social media account on the contact screen) open your browser or the relevant app, which are governed by those services’ own privacy policies.

4. Retention and deletion

The calculated statistics are stored in the App’s storage on your device until you upload a new file, which replaces them. To delete them, uninstall the App; this removes the App’s data from your device. Copies in device backups may remain until you delete the backup, and on Android they may be restored if you reinstall the App. Because the Developer holds no data about you, there is nothing to request deletion of and no consent to withdraw. If you have turned on iCloud or Google backup, App data on your device may be included in those backups, which are kept in your own Apple or Google account and are under your control; the Developer cannot access them.

5. Security

App data is stored in the protected area the operating system gives each app, which other apps cannot normally access. We recommend using a screen lock. The ZIP file you downloaded from Instagram stays in its download folder after you upload it; it may contain private information, so we recommend deleting it when you no longer need it.

6. Children

The App is not directed at children. Instagram’s own age requirement applies to creating an Instagram account. The App does not collect personal data from anyone, including children.

7. Your rights under KVKK

The Developer does not access data processed in the App and does not process it for its own purposes; it is processed only on your device, under your control. When you email the Developer or visit the website, the KVKK Privacy Notice applies. You can send any question or request about your rights under Article 11 of Turkish Personal Data Protection Law No. 6698 to info@furkanerdogan.com.

8. No affiliation with Meta or Instagram

SocialSense is not affiliated with, endorsed, approved or sponsored by Meta Platforms, Inc. or Instagram. The App does not log in to your Instagram account and does not connect to Instagram’s systems.

9. Changes

This policy may be updated. The current version is always published on this page with the effective date at the top updated. Major changes affecting how the App handles data are also announced in the App’s update notes or inside the App.
