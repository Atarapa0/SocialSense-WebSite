Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 3, 2026

This Privacy Policy applies to the SocialSense mobile application (“SocialSense” or the “Application”) developed by Furkan Erdoğan and to the website associated with SocialSense.

SocialSense is an independent application designed to help users analyze their own Instagram data export archives on their devices.

SocialSense is not affiliated with, endorsed by, sponsored by, or associated with Instagram or Meta Platforms, Inc. Instagram and Meta are trademarks of their respective owners.

1. How SocialSense Works

SocialSense does not log in to your Instagram account and does not request your Instagram username or password as login credentials.

The Application:

  • does not log in to your Instagram or Meta account;
  • does not use the Instagram/Meta Graph API;
  • does not scrape data from your Instagram account;
  • does not request an Instagram access token;
  • does not identify who has viewed your profile;
  • does not continuously or live-monitor your Instagram account.

To perform an analysis, you select a supported ZIP/JSON archive that you obtained using Instagram’s data export functionality.

2. Processing of Your Instagram Data Archive

The Instagram data archive you select is processed on your device for analysis.

Your Instagram ZIP archive is not uploaded to SocialSense servers for analysis.

Depending on the structure and contents of your archive, SocialSense may process the following types of data on your device:

  • your Instagram username and, where available in the archive, your display name;
  • your followers and accounts you follow;
  • follow relationships and associated dates;
  • like records;
  • comment records;
  • saved-content records;
  • interests and topic information;
  • close friends lists;
  • sent and received follow requests;
  • supported story-like records;
  • conversation and interaction information required for analysis from message archives;
  • counters and summary information relating to sent and received messages and supported Reels interactions.

These data are processed on your device to provide SocialSense’s analysis features.

3. Message Data

If your Instagram data archive contains message data, SocialSense may process the relevant message JSON files on your device to generate supported analyses.

Message files may be read into memory during analysis. Full message text is not retained in SocialSense’s persistent local analysis record. Instead, account or participant information associated with conversations and counters or summary information relating to messages and supported Reels interactions may be stored.

This processing takes place on your device, and your message archive is not uploaded to SocialSense servers for analysis.

4. Data Stored on Your Device

To avoid having to analyze your archive again every time the Application is opened, parsed analysis data may be stored in the Application’s local storage on your device.

Depending on the contents of your archive, this local record may include information such as usernames, follow relationships, interaction records, comment information, saved content, interests, close friends, follow requests, and message/Reels analysis summaries.

Application preferences such as theme, language, the last data-update time, and acknowledged Application notices may also be stored on your device.

SocialSense does not claim to apply an additional custom application-level encryption layer to these data. Storage and security mechanisms provided by your device’s operating system may separately apply.

5. Temporary ZIP Files

SocialSense does not upload the Instagram archive you select to a permanent server-side archive.

However, your device’s operating system or system file picker may create a temporary local copy of the ZIP file you select within the Application’s temporary storage. Temporary local copies may be created particularly as part of file-selection behavior on iOS.

Management of these temporary files is partly controlled by the operating system and file-picker components.

SocialSense does not delete the original Instagram ZIP file that you selected from Files, Downloads, iCloud Drive, or another location.

6. Analytics, Advertising, and Tracking

In the current version of the SocialSense mobile application, the following are not actively used:

  • third-party usage analytics services;
  • active advertising SDKs for displaying advertisements;
  • advertising-based user tracking;
  • attribution SDKs;
  • IDFA/IDFV-based advertising tracking;
  • Firebase Analytics;
  • Google Analytics;
  • Meta Pixel;
  • third-party remote crash-reporting services such as Crashlytics or Sentry.

SocialSense performs statistical analyses of your Instagram archive. These local Application analyses do not mean that user behavior is being transmitted to a third-party analytics service.

This section describes the technical behavior of the current Application version. If new services that affect data processing are introduced in the future, this Privacy Policy will be updated accordingly.

7. Website

Visiting the SocialSense website is a separate data-processing activity from analyzing your Instagram archive in the mobile Application.

When the website is delivered to you, the web server, hosting/security infrastructure, and content-delivery services may technically process connection information including:

  • your IP address;
  • the page or resource requested;
  • the date and time of the request;
  • standard HTTP information sent by your browser;
  • technical information required for connection, security, and error diagnosis.

The SocialSense website may use Cloudflare infrastructure. Depending on the production configuration, Cloudflare may process technical connection information for website delivery, security, and network/error diagnostics.

While the website uses externally hosted fonts, icons, or similar third-party resources, your browser may connect directly to the providers of those resources. In that case, your IP address and standard connection information may technically be received by the relevant provider.

These website infrastructure connections do not mean that your Instagram ZIP archive is sent to SocialSense servers.

8. Cookies and Similar Technologies

The current SocialSense website does not use its own analytics or advertising cookie system to track user behavior for advertising or profiling purposes.

However, Cloudflare or other third-party infrastructure providers used by the website may use their own technologies for security, network management, or technical service delivery. Data processing by those providers may be subject to their respective privacy policies.

If SocialSense introduces non-essential analytics, advertising, or similar tracking technologies in the future, this Privacy Policy will be updated and any consent or permission mechanisms required by applicable law will be implemented.

9. Support and Email Communications

The SocialSense website does not provide a web form through which users can upload their Instagram archives.

If you choose to contact us by email, the information processed may include, depending on what you send:

  • your email address;
  • your name or display name;
  • the contents of your message;
  • files or other information you voluntarily attach;
  • standard communication metadata generated by email services.

This information may be used to respond to your request, provide support, and, where necessary, maintain communication security or comply with legal obligations.

Please do not send your Instagram ZIP archive, password, or other sensitive information by email unless it is necessary for your support request.

10. External Links and Opening Instagram Profiles

SocialSense may open external websites or applications as a result of an action you take.

For example, when you tap an Instagram username in the Application, SocialSense may attempt to open the relevant profile in the Instagram application or on instagram.com. In doing so, the Instagram username to be opened may be passed to the destination application or URL.

Subsequent processing by Instagram, Apple, Google, email providers, and other third-party services is governed by their own terms and privacy policies.

SocialSense does not control the data-processing practices of those third parties.

11. Sharing and Sale of Data

SocialSense does not sell the Instagram ZIP archive analyzed on your device to advertisers.

SocialSense does not upload your Instagram ZIP archive to its servers for analysis and does not transfer that archive to third-party analytics or advertising services for analysis.

The following activities are separate from the local analysis of your Instagram archive:

  • technical connection data processed by website infrastructure providers when you visit the website;
  • opening a third-party link at your request;
  • opening an Instagram profile externally;
  • communicating with us by email;
  • compliance with a legal obligation where required by law.

12. Data Retention

Analysis data generated from your Instagram archive may remain stored locally on your device until you delete it, use the relevant data-clearing function in the Application, or the data is otherwise removed through the Application or operating system.

Because SocialSense does not store your Instagram ZIP archive on its own servers for analysis, there is no SocialSense server-side retention period for that archive.

If you contact us by email for support, communication records may be retained by the email service for as long as reasonably necessary to respond to your request, prevent abuse, or comply with applicable legal obligations.

Retention periods for technical connection records maintained by website infrastructure providers may depend on the relevant provider’s configuration and policies.

13. Data Deletion

SocialSense does not operate a SocialSense user-account system, so there is no online SocialSense user account to delete.

The Application’s data-clearing functionality may be used to clear local analysis data generated by SocialSense.

Under the current Application behavior, this action is intended to clear the Application’s primary local analysis record and associated last-update information.

However, this action may not automatically delete:

  • the original Instagram ZIP file stored in Files, Downloads, iCloud Drive, or another location;
  • every temporary file that may have been created by the operating system or file picker;
  • all Application preferences, such as theme or language;
  • backups that may have been created by the operating system or external services.

Uninstalling the Application may cause Application data stored on the device to be removed as managed by the operating system; however, SocialSense does not delete the original Instagram ZIP file stored in your own file storage.

To request deletion of personal information that you sent to us by email and that we retain, contact furkaan.er.34@gmail.com. Information that must be retained by law may be excluded from deletion.

14. Nature and Privacy-Relevant Limitations of Analyses

Analyses generated by SocialSense are based on the information contained in the Instagram data export archive selected by the user.

SocialSense:

  • does not continuously monitor your Instagram account;
  • cannot determine who has viewed your profile;
  • does not identify real-time unfollow events unless a separate historical comparison capability exists;
  • does not have access to private profile-visitor data supplied by Instagram or Meta.

Some analyses are statistical or approximate results derived from interaction records in the archive. They should not be understood as live account metrics verified by Instagram.

15. Children’s Privacy

SocialSense is not specifically designed for children.

Users who require parental or legal-guardian consent for the processing of personal data under applicable law should comply with the legal requirements applicable to them when using the Application and related services.

If you believe a child’s personal information has been improperly sent to us through a communication channel such as email, please contact us at furkaan.er.34@gmail.com.

16. Security

SocialSense is designed to perform analysis of the Instagram data archive on the user’s device where possible, reducing the need to transmit the archive to developer-controlled servers for analysis.

However, no device, storage system, internet connection, or method of electronic communication can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

You are encouraged to use your device’s security features and keep your Instagram data archive in a secure location to help protect your archive and locally generated analysis data.

17. Your Rights

Depending on your location and applicable law, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, restrict the processing of, or exercise other data-protection rights in relation to personal data actually retained by us.

Because Instagram analysis data that remains only on your device is not uploaded to SocialSense servers, the developer does not have remote access to those local data. Such data are therefore primarily managed through the Application’s data-management functions or through your device.

For requests concerning information retained by us, such as support communications, contact furkaan.er.34@gmail.com.

SocialSense does not sell or share the Instagram ZIP archive analyzed on your device for advertising purposes.

Requests may be submitted to furkaan.er.34@gmail.com.

19. International Data Processing

The core analysis of your Instagram ZIP archive performed by SocialSense takes place on your device and is not transferred to SocialSense servers for analysis.

However, when you use website infrastructure, third-party links, or email communications, the relevant service providers may process data in different countries. Such processing may be subject to those providers’ infrastructure, terms, and privacy policies.
